Privacy Policy — Friday

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Who uses the app

Friday is a private assistant used only by its owner. No other people sign in to it.

What data it accesses

Only the owner's own Google account data, and only the services the owner granted: Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Sheets and Google Drive.

How the data is used

Solely to perform tasks the owner asks for: reading the schedule, creating reminders and tasks, and saving meeting recordings, transcripts and notes to the owner's Drive.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's private server. Data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. Google user data is not used to train AI models.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
